# ExplosiveManeater
take care of the maneater or it will wipe every player in the lobby
i have no idea why you would wanna download this but here's how this works:
If a maneater spawns, you'll get a tip box on the side of your screen telling you one spawned
If a maneater cries, you'll get a tip box on the side of your screen telling you its crying
same if it stops crying
if its about to transform, the maneater will puke (vanilla behavior) and the mod will warn you
you can reset its growth meter by feeding him **any** item and yes, any is ANY, even the clipboard of the ship
Who needs it: everyone in the lobby.
